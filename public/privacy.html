Memory Cards Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-05-27

Memory Cards is designed as a local flashcard learning app.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

User-created decks, cards, review schedules, and review statistics are stored locally on the user's device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking services.

Speech

The app may use the device's built-in text-to-speech functionality to pronounce English words or phrases.

Data Storage

All learning content is stored locally on the device. Deleting the app may delete the locally stored data.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page.
